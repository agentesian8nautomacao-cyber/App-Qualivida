PROTOCOLO HMAC
============================================================
Canonical (LF):
  v1
  {timestamp}
  {METHOD}
  {path_with_query}
  {body_sha256_hex}
  {organization_id}
  {condominium_id}
  {idempotency_key_or_empty}

Signature = hex(HMAC-SHA256(secret, canonical))
Compare = timingSafeEqual

  X-Sentinela-Client-Id
  X-Sentinela-Timestamp
  X-Sentinela-Signature
  X-Organization-Id
  X-Condominium-Id
  Idempotency-Key (opcional no G2)
